# Onboarding, permissions and Settings

Read this before changing `src/onboarding.rs`, `src/settings.rs`, `src/command_line.rs` or the helper's `Onboarding.swift`, `PermissionPanel.swift` and `Settings.swift`.

## Onboarding (`src/onboarding.rs`, `helper/Onboarding.swift`)

Rust decides what shows (`Flow`, `launch_plan`, `Gate`, tested) and stores progress under `"onboarding"` in `~/.config/scrawl/config.json`, including the tour's current step (`resume`), so any quit (macOS's "Quit & Reopen" too) picks up there; the SwiftUI window in `scrawl-helper onboarding` only renders the state it's sent and reports buttons (`grant:<permission>` for a row).

Tour: Welcome → HowItWorks → Permissions (one checklist of three required rows, Screen Recording first, then Microphone and Accessibility; any order works) → TryIt → Done. Camera (iPhone) is never in the tour: its first use opens `Flow::fix(vec![Camera])`, the same screen with one row.

HowItWorks is a silent 11 s loop (`helper/OnboardingDemo.swift`): a pretend checkout page whose Pay button runs out of its window, ⌥⇧S opening the pill, subtitles for what the person says, a ⇧ mark around the bug, a click, ⌥⇧S to Copied, the frame lifting off as a one-frame contact sheet with its `1` chip, ⌥⇧V into an agent chat, and the fix. Everything on the stage is a function of one clock (`DemoScript`), so any moment renders the same. Holding ⇧ over the stage pauses the loop and draws a mark in the window (nothing is recorded or asked for). The button under it reads Skip until the loop has played through or a mark was drawn, then Continue; the helper reports `skip`, `watched` and `practiced`, and `Flow` keeps the furthest outcome in `Progress::tutorial` (`"tutorial"`), so a resumed tour keeps Continue. Reduced motion shows four still frames with captions. `scrawl-helper onboarding --render DIR --demo-frames 30` writes one loop as PNGs for a video.

Input Monitoring is never asked for or read (macOS wants a relaunch after it; the enum variant stays only so 0.3.x configs load, and `input_monitoring_is_never_requested` greps the sources): clicks come from an `NSEvent` global mouse monitor (no permission), ⇧ and held modifiers from `CGEventSourceFlagsState` (no permission), keys from an active `kCGEventTapOptionDefault` tap (Accessibility), which the HID poller makes or stops every 500 ms as `AXIsProcessTrusted` changes (`check_key_tap`; live, no relaunch). Never poll key codes (`CGEventSourceKeyState`) or make a listen-only tap.

Grants are re-read every 0.5 s from a fresh `scrawl-helper permissions`; the coordinator caches them (`ScrawlCoordinator::grants`).

macOS shows "Quit & Reopen / Later" the moment Screen Recording is granted, and a running process may not see the grant (`CGPreflightScreenCaptureAccess` caches). So once the helper says granted, `read_grants` tries a one-frame ScreenCaptureKit capture in this process (`capture::screen_capture_usable(true)`); if it fails, `Flow::tick` shows "Reopening Scrawl" for `RELAUNCH_AFTER_MS` and returns `Effect::Relaunch`, and the coordinator saves `Progress::relaunching` (tour `resume` = Permissions, or `reopenFix`) and `onboarding::relaunch()` reopens the app (without `-n`) after this pid exits. Quit & Reopen lands in the same place through the saved `resume`; a tour resumed on Permissions stays there. `relaunchedAt` stops a second automatic relaunch within `RELAUNCH_GUARD_MS`; the row then offers Reopen Scrawl. `scrawl sck-probe [--watch S]` / `scrawl-helper sck-probe` test whether a grant reaches a running process.

Until the required ones are on, ⌥⇧S and the menu's record items start nothing (no picker or overlay; the picker sits at `NSStatusWindowLevel + 1` and takes clicks, which trapped macOS prompts under it): `Gate` holds the request and `tick_gate` opens or raises the window once the modifiers are up. Capture code must not call `CGRequestScreenCaptureAccess` or start the iPhone mirror without Camera: go through `ready_to_record` / `ready_for`.

When a row turns on, the window comes forward once macOS stops asking (no UserNotificationCenter or SecurityAgent window, no System Settings alert in front such as its Quit & Reopen; `returnAfterGrant`, for five minutes after the grant). Once Screen Recording works on Permissions or Try it, `Effect::ConfirmScreen` lists the screen once (after those questions, 1.2 s after the window is back) so ScreenCaptureKit's "bypass the system private window picker" alert comes up there, under a note saying to choose Allow (`View::picker_note`), rather than over the first recording.

Render every screen and permission state with `scrawl-helper onboarding --render DIR`.

Rows for draggable lists (`Permission::draggable`: Screen Recording, Accessibility) go straight to `OpenSettings`, which launches `scrawl-helper permission-panel` (`helper/PermissionPanel.swift`) from the row's button: the card flies to just below System Settings (inside its empty lower area when the screen has no room; `PermissionPanel.dock`, checked by `scripts/check-permission-panel-dock.sh`), drags the `Scrawl.app` that contains the helper, reads grants from a fresh `--check` process (never trusts the drop), and is told `granted` when the row turns on. Preview it with `permission-panel --permission screen-recording --preview`; never `tccutil reset` Kit's real grants to test it.

## Settings (`src/settings.rs`, `helper/Settings.swift`)

The onboarding pattern again. `scrawl-helper settings` draws the `View` it's sent (General, Recording, Storage, Permissions, Agents, About; ⌘, or the menu's **Settings…**, and **Open Settings** on onboarding's last screen) and reports actions as JSON lines; `Panel` decides and writes `~/.config/scrawl/config.json` through `handoff::set_config_value_in` (other keys and `0600` kept).

Both shortcuts (`ShortcutKind::Record` ⌥⇧S, `Paste` ⌥⇧V) live under `"shortcuts"` (Carbon `keyCode`/`modifiers`/`label`; absent is the default, and `"paste": null` turns paste off; 0.3.x's file under Application Support is moved across once). Each shows only its keys, as rounded keycaps with a little depth in a recessed field, like Hex's control (`helper/ShortcutKeys.swift`). Holding its modifiers while Settings is in front sinks those keys onto their bases and turns the key red. Listening tints the field red: a dot breathes beside "Type a shortcut", and modifiers land as they go down (in macOS order) and lift out when let go, with a red caret after them. An accepted shortcut's keys bob once, left to right. A refused one turns red and shakes before the old keys return, with Rust's reason in the caption. Every value is a closed-form spring evaluated at a time (`Track`, `KeyController`), so the helper draws any moment the same live or rendered, and the TimelineView stops when the keys are still. Clicking a shortcut's row sends `record-shortcut`: Rust pauses every Scrawl hotkey (`capture::pause_shortcuts`: Carbon unregistered, `emit_*` muted) so the current combo reaches the recorder, then `settings::validate` turns down combos without ⌘/⌥/⌃ (function keys excepted), ⌘ + one key, macOS's own (a static list plus the helper's enabled `CopySymbolicHotKeys`) and the other shortcut; `capture::set_shortcuts` registers both and puts the old ones back if `RegisterEventHotKey` refuses. Only a default answers to its aliases (⌃/⌘ added, ⌃⌥⌘, the ANSI key); a custom combo matches exactly in Carbon and the key event tap. Paste can be turned off (`ShortcutKind::can_unset`; Record can't, it's the way in) with the × beside its field or Delete while it listens (`clear-shortcut`, `Pressed::is_delete`): `ShortcutConfig::unset()` registers nothing and matches no key, and the field shows "Set shortcut".

Recording › Microphone (`src/mic.rs`): `"microphone": {"id","name"}` holds the CoreAudio device UID (the same string as `AVCaptureDevice.uniqueID`; the name only names a missing device), absent = System default. `mic::Watch` (CoreAudio listeners on the device list and default input, own thread, bursts coalesced) re-lists inputs into `Event::Microphones` while Settings is open; nothing polls. `AudioRecorder` opens the chosen device on its worker thread, else the default, and a missing or unopenable choice never stops a capture (`mic::Pick`: the note in Settings, one log line via `note_pick`); an error partway (unplugged, overload) keeps the samples heard so far. The iPhone mirror gets `--microphone` for its narration. The helper's `MicMeter` (AVCaptureSession, levels from the connection's channels) runs only while the Recording pane is on screen and `meter` is set, which Rust does only once Microphone is granted. `scrawl mic list|set <id|default>|watch`.

Storage › Location (`CapturesDirView`): Change… opens the helper's folder picker and sends `set capturesDir` with an absolute path; Reset sends `""`, which removes the key. `handoff::default_captures_dir` reads it on every call (`SCRAWL_CAPTURES_DIR` wins, then `"capturesDir"`, then `builtin_captures_dir`; `~` expands, a relative path is logged and skipped; tests never read the config), so new captures, retention, `scrawl latest` and the size all follow at once. Captures already made aren't moved. A folder that can't be created or written falls back to the built-in one (logged once). State kept beside the captures (`inbox.json`, `last-mode`, legacy `config.json`) stays under `builtin_captures_dir`'s parent. The legacy config path stays under `builtin_captures_dir`.

Recording › Language shows under Transcription while it's Apple Speech: Automatic, then the helper's supported `locales` by name. It writes `appleLocale` (Automatic removes the key) and returns `Effect::SpeechModel`, which restarts the model check for the new language (see `docs/keyframes-and-text.md`).

The sidebar is the helper's alone: a row selects on mouse-down and its pane replaces the last on the next frame, with no transition (keep it that way; a fade read as lag; the level meter starts after the Recording pane has drawn). Rust's `state` never carries the pane (only `show` does, when Settings is opened again), so a reply can't move the selection.

Launch at login is `SMAppService.mainApp` (only inside Scrawl.app). There's no Dock icon: gpui 0.2.2 makes the app Regular after launch despite `LSUIElement`, so `ui::stay_out_of_dock` sets Accessory again at the top of `run_app`. The menu keeps Record, Choose What to Record…, Open Latest Capture, Export (flag), Settings…, Check for Updates…, Quit.

Agents › Command-line tool (`src/command_line.rs`) symlinks the app's own binary (its real path, which is how it finds `scrawl-helper`) into the first writable of `/opt/homebrew/bin`, `/usr/local/bin`, else `~/.local/bin`; it never asks for admin, never touches a `scrawl` that doesn't link to a Scrawl.app, and the Homebrew cask's `binary` link reads as installed. A bare `scrawl` from a terminal prints help instead of starting a second app. Agents › Agent skill shows `bunx skills add kitlangton/scrawl` (`skills/scrawl/SKILL.md`, also printed by `scrawl skill`); keep that file true to the CLI.

Render every pane (dark, light, each shortcut state: held, listening, partial, refused, accepted, custom; microphone chosen/missing/not allowed) with `scrawl-helper settings --render DIR`, and one take on the shortcuts as 60 fps PNGs for a video with `--render DIR --shortcut-clip [--light]`; test with `cargo test settings::`.

Under `"ui": "rune"` both windows are drawn by Rune in Scrawl's own process instead of the helper (`src/ui/rune_settings.rs`, `src/ui/rune_onboarding.rs`; `Transport::InProcess`); Rust's side (`Panel`, `Flow`, the events) is the same. `scrawl app --scene settings-<state>|onboarding-<state> --snap out.png` renders one state headless, and `uv run scripts/check-rune-windows.py [--sheet DIR]` renders every state both ways and fails when Rune strays from SwiftUI's render. The Screen Recording and Accessibility rows still open the SwiftUI permission panel.

## Lower other audio while recording (`src/playback.rs`, `helper/PlaybackVolume.swift`)

Off by default. General › Lower other audio while recording (`"lowerPlayback": true`) ramps the default output device's volume over one second to `playbackLevel` percent of what it was (Lower to, default 20%; 0 mutes) from the moment the shortcut is pressed or a recording starts, Mac or cabled iPhone, and back when it finishes, is cancelled or the picker closes without recording. `scrawl-helper playback-volume --server`, started at launch while the setting is on, does the CoreAudio work and takes `start N` / `stop` lines on stdin. It reads every writable output channel's volume scalar before writing any (the master volume only when the channels have none), so balance and silent channels come back as they were; it never touches the mute flag or any input device. It restores when told to stop, when Scrawl exits or crashes (stdin closes) and at once on SIGTERM, SIGINT or SIGHUP; it leaves the volume alone if someone changed it during the recording, and a device switched to mid-recording isn't lowered (the original device is restored). Fixed-volume outputs are left unchanged. Windows and Linux do nothing. `scrawl-helper playback-volume --self-test` runs the ramp tests on fake devices; `--inspect` prints the current device's volume controls without changing them.
